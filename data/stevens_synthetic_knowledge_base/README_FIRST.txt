STEVENS INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY — SYNTHETIC KNOWLEDGE BASE
===============================================================

ALL FILES IN THIS PACKAGE ARE SYNTHETIC TEST DATA.
They are NOT official Stevens Institute of Technology documents and must not
be presented as authentic university policies, requirements, tuition rates,
academic calendars, or legal/immigration guidance.

Purpose
-------
This 20-document corpus is designed for study/testing of a university
knowledge-base or RAG system. It intentionally includes:
- similar requirements across different programs;
- two academic years of tuition and calendars;
- current and prior versions of the same handbook/admissions material;
- policies with overlapping terminology;
- exact course codes and prerequisite relationships;
- questions where the system should prefer a more specific source.

Provenance
----------
No document below should be treated as a publicly sourced Stevens document.
The titles and dates are synthetic labels created for testing. For a real
knowledge base, replace these files with documents downloaded directly from
official Stevens Institute of Technology sources, while preserving the
original title, publication/effective date, URL, and retrieval date.
